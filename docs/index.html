01 Timing is everything

Visible windows require the ISS to be sunlit, your Sun to be at least 6° below the horizon, and the station to be at least 10° above it.

02 Green means a better chance

Best: visible elevation ≥40° and clouds ≤30% when weather is available. Partial: visible elevation ≥10° and clouds ≤70%. Higher cloud cover is poor. Without weather, colors describe geometry only.

03 A forecast, not a promise

Days 8–30 have no weather forecast. Orbital estimates become less reliable farther ahead, particularly after 14 days. Recheck before heading out. Buildings, terrain, and local cloud patches can affect your view.
